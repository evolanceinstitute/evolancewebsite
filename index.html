Women-First Tech Initiative

Capstone Ignite

Empowering Women Through Technology · Founded by NoorAbbas

25 Class Days | 10+ Real Skills | 1 Recognized Certificate

Why Capstone Ignite Exists

Millions of women have smartphones and laptops, but lack a structured, safe, and supportive environment to build digital confidence or income. Capstone Ignite was built from scratch as a women-first IT program where every topic connects directly to independence and real-world income.

Designed for Every Woman

The Student (Age 16–25)

Wants IT skills to stand out in university or prepare for future employment.

The Homemaker

Wants to earn online from home without sacrificing household responsibilities.

The Job Seeker

Needs digital confidence (MS Office, Gmail, Canva) expected by modern employers.

25-Day 4-Phase Curriculum Breakdown

Phase 1

Foundations (Days 1–6)

Computer hardware basics, Windows navigation, file management, typing accuracy, and sitting confidently at any PC.

Phase 2

Productivity (Days 7–14)

MS Word document formatting, Excel spreadsheets, PowerPoint presentations, Google Workspace, and Canva graphic design.

Phase 3

Digital Skills & AI (Days 15–20)

Safe browsing, online privacy, ChatGPT & Gemini AI prompt engineering, social media content creation.

Phase 4

Earn & Grow (Days 21–25)

Setting up freelancing profiles on Fiverr & Upwork, pricing services, writing proposals, delivering client projects, and graduation.

Program Highlights

  • Dedicated laptop for every student
  • Safe, supportive environment
  • Small batch sizes (personal attention)
  • Recognized Certificate & Portfolio

Capstone Juniors

Next-Generation Technology & Coding Programme

30 Days | 4 Phases | 15+ Skills | 3 Projects

Building Problem-Solving Logic for Life

Technology isn't just about screen time — it's about building computational thinking, problem-solving skills, and AI literacy that give young minds a genuine head start in life.

30-Day 4-Phase Curriculum Breakdown

Phase 1

IT Foundations (Days 1–5)

Understanding computer parts, Windows settings, taskbar shortcuts, file/folder organization, and proper typing technique.

Phase 2

Internet & Productivity (Days 6–10)

Safe online browsing, phishing awareness, MS Word document creation, PowerPoint & designing a take-home Canva poster.

Phase 3

Coding & Logic (Days 11–15)

Computational thinking, Scratch drag-and-drop block coding, Python language intro, building and presenting a coded Scratch game.

Phase 4

AI & Showcase (Days 16–20)

How AI works, writing clear AI prompts, building a final showcase project, and presenting to parents on Certificate Day.

What Students Walk Away With

  • Real computer confidence
  • A designed creative Canva project
  • Their own coded Scratch game
  • Python programming basics
  • Certificate & Parent Presentation

Master IT Program

A Complete Workplace & Career Breakdown for Students & Parents

11 Modules | 60+ Practical Skills | Real Workplace Tools

11 Modules Breakdown

Module 1 & 2

Computer Fundamentals & Operating Systems

Hardware parts (CPU, RAM, Storage), specs reading, Windows navigation, file compression, software safety, and keyboard shortcuts.

Module 3 & 4

MS Office & Google Workspace

Formatting Word documents, Excel formulas/spreadsheets, PowerPoint slides, Gmail setup, Docs, Sheets, Drive collaboration, and professional email rules.

Module 5 & 6

Internet, Networks & Cybersecurity

LAN/WAN/WiFi, DNS, virus & threat protection, 2FA/OTPs, anti-phishing defense, and data protection — a critical life skill.

Module 7 & 8

Databases & AI Tools for Real Life

Flat vs relational databases, spreadsheet record keeping, prompt writing for ChatGPT & Gemini, homework research, and responsible AI use.

Module 9 & 10

Programming & Web Development

HTML web structure, Scratch logic, Python fundamentals, C/C++ programming basics, building websites with WordPress & Webflow, SEO & live hosting.

Module 11

Creative Design & Freelancing

Canva social graphics, posters, card design, setting up Fiverr & Upwork profiles, proposal writing, pricing services, and client communication.

Master IT Summary

Produces well-rounded graduates capable of working in offices, coding software, managing websites, and earning online.
